Happy Birthday 🎂

Fatimah Ahmad Muhammad
25 October 2002

We do not get to choose our families, but we do get to choose our friends. Somehow, you managed to become closer to me than family, and for that, I wish you every happiness, blessing, and goodness this life has to offer. Happy birthday, bestie! 🎂❤️

Congratulations! You’ve survived another year on this planet. Soon, you’ll be 25, then 30, 35, 40, and before you know it, you’ll be complaining about back pain while explaining to younger people how life was better in your days. 😭😂

And if Allah wills, you’ll achieve your dreams, build a beautiful life, get married, raise wonderful children, watch them grow up and get married, and eventually become that adorable grandmother who spoils her grandchildren and pretends she doesn’t. Perhaps you’ll even live long enough to see your great-grandchildren. 🥹❤️

But let’s be realistic. Life doesn’t always follow the script. You might spend your youth stressing over things that won’t matter in five years, overthinking messages that deserved no reply, and fighting battles that you’ll eventually laugh about. You might even trip over absolutely nothing one day and leave this world wondering why the floor had personal issues with you. 😭💀

Regardless of how beautifully or chaotically your story unfolds, one thing remains certain: our time in this world is limited. Every birthday is another year behind us and, if Allah grants us life, another year of opportunities, lessons, laughter, and memories ahead.

So, as your beloved friend, I would like to offer my heartfelt condolences on the unfortunate reality that you are, once again, one year closer to your eventual departure from this world. 🥀😭

Unfortunately, I cannot guarantee that we’ll still be in contact by the time you finally clock out of this dunya. We might be arguing over something ridiculous, living in different countries, or you might have become too rich and famous to remember the poor people who knew you before the fame. 💔😂

Therefore, in anticipation of that inevitable day, I would like to express my grief in advance. Please accept my deepest condolences on your future demise. You will be missed terribly, assuming, of course, that I am still alive to miss you.

May Allah grant you a long life filled with barakah, protect you from harm, bless your future, forgive your shortcomings, grant you goodness in this world and the Hereafter, and reunite us in Jannatul Firdaus. Ameen. 🤲🏽❤️

Happy birthday, Fatimah Ahmad Muhammad. Enjoy your remaining years on this temporary planet. Your countdown continues. 🎂💀😭❤️
